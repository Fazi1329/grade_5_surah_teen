# The Surah At-Tin Adventure! 🍃
**Interactive Grade 5 Islamic Studies Quiz & Learning Game**

*“Learn, Play, Answer & Become a Qur'an Champion!”*

---

## 🌟 Overview
**The Surah At-Tin Adventure!** is a fully interactive, vibrant, and educational Islamic Studies quiz website crafted specifically for Grade 5 students (ages 10–11). Designed to feel like an exciting learning journey rather than a dull exam, students embark on a 4-stage quest through the verses, vocabulary, and moral reflections of **Surah At-Tin (Surah 95)**.

---

## 🎨 Theme & Visual Design
- **Harmonious Islamic Color Palette:** Emerald Green, Sky Blue, Radiant Gold, and Pure White.
- **Visual Garden Setting:** Features a welcoming custom illustration depicting a peaceful Islamic garden with lush fig and olive trees under a sunny crescent sky.
- **Islamic Motifs & Typography:** Elegant Arabic typography (`Amiri` and `Scheherazade New`) paired with child-friendly display fonts (`Fredoka` and `Outfit`).
- **Dynamic Micro-Interactions:** Custom canvas confetti particle celebrations, bouncing star badges, glowing pulse effects, and responsive cards.
- **Fully Responsive:** Adapts seamlessly across desktop monitors, interactive classroom projectors, iPads/tablets, and mobile phones.

---

## 🕹️ 4 Interactive Rounds (20 Questions Total • 200 Points)

### 🧭 Round 1: Qur'an Explorer (Multiple Choice)
- **5 Questions** testing knowledge of the Surah's name, revelation background (Makki), number of verses (8 ayat), blessed foods (figs and olives), prophets, and the creation of humanity in the best mould (*Ahsan Taqweem*).
- 4 clear options per question with instant encouraging feedback and explanations.

### 🤲 Round 2: Match It! (Drag-and-Drop & Tap-to-Match)
- Connects 5 sacred Arabic phrases with their English meanings:
  1. **وَالتِّينِ** ⟷ *By the fig*
  2. **وَالزَّيْتُونِ** ⟷ *And the olive*
  3. **أَحْسَنِ تَقْوِيمٍ** ⟷ *The best form*
  4. **أَسْفَلَ سَافِلِينَ** ⟷ *The lowest of the low*
  5. **أَجْرٌ غَيْرُ مَمْنُونٍ** ⟷ *A reward that does not end*
- Supports both HTML5 desktop drag-and-drop and mobile/tablet tap-to-match with animated matching indicators and chimes. Shuffles on every playthrough!

### ✍️ Round 3: Fill in the Missing Words
- **5 Questions** completing authentic verses from Surah At-Tin:
  1. وَالتِّينِ وَ________ *(الزَّيْتُونِ)*
  2. لَقَدْ خَلَقْنَا الْإِنسَانَ فِي أَحْسَنِ ________ *(تَقْوِيمٍ)*
  3. ثُمَّ رَدَدْنَاهُ أَسْفَلَ ________ *(سَافِلِينَ)*
  4. إِلَّا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا وَعَمِلُوا ________ *(الصَّالِحَاتِ)*
  5. فَمَا يُكَذِّبُكَ بَعْدُ بِالدِّينِ أَلَيْسَ اللَّهُ بِأَحْكَمِ ________ *(الْحَاكِمِينَ)*
- Interactive clickable **Word Bank** with transliteration clues, plus optional typing with diacritic-tolerant smart Arabic normalization.

### 🌟 Round 4: Think Like a Qur'an Champion (Reflections & Lessons)
- **5 Age-Appropriate Questions** exploring practical life applications:
  - Recognizing our divine gifts, health, and upright stature (*Ahsan Taqweem*).
  - Showing gratitude through daily righteous actions.
  - Linking faith (*Iman*) with good deeds (*Amal Salih*).
  - The promise of continuous reward (*Ajrun ghayru mamnoon*) in Jannah.
  - Daily habits for Grade 5 students (punctual Salah, honesty, kind speech).

## 🎮 Special Game Features
- ⭐ **Points & Stars:** 10 points per question (200 total max) + 4 collectible bonus stars (1 per completed round).
- 🔊 **Built-in Web Audio Synthesizer:** Zero external sound dependencies! Harmonic correct arpeggios, gentle mistake chords, match dings, and victory fanfare with instant Mute/Unmute toggle.
- 💡 **Friendly Champion Clues:** Optional Hint button on every question offering context clues without spoiling the answer.
- 🎯 **Live HUD:** Shows current round badge, score, progress bar, and question counter.
- 🔄 **Targeted Practice Mode:** Retry only the questions you missed with a single click.
- 📖 **Interactive Surah Reader:** Built-in drawer with full Arabic text, verse-by-verse transliteration, and English translation.

  - Review student session performance.

  - Print/export classroom score report.
