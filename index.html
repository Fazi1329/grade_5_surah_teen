📖 Surah At-Tin (The Fig) — سورة التين

Surah 95 • 8 Verses (Ayat) • Revealed in Makkah
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ In the name of Allah, the Entirely Merciful, the Especially Merciful.
1

وَالتِّينِ وَالزَّيْتُونِ ﴿١﴾

Wat-teeni waz-zaytoon

"By the fig and the olive,"

2

وَطُورِ سِينِينَ ﴿٢﴾

Wa toori seeneen

"And [by] Mount Sinai,"

3

وَهَٰذَا الْبَلَدِ الْأَمِينِ ﴿٣﴾

Wa haazal baladil ameen

"And [by] this secure city [Makkah],"

4

لَقَدْ خَلَقْنَا الْإِنسَانَ فِي أَحْسَنِ تَقْوِيمٍ ﴿٤﴾

Laqad khalaqnal insaana fee ahsani taqweem

"We have certainly created man in the best stature / form;"

5

ثُمَّ رَدَدْنَاهُ أَسْفَلَ سَافِلِينَ ﴿٥﴾

Thumma radadnaahu asfala saafileen

"Then We return him to the lowest of the low,"

6

إِلَّا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا وَعَمِلُوا الصَّالِحَاتِ فَلَهُمْ أَجْرٌ غَيْرُ مَمْنُونٍ ﴿٦﴾

Illal lazeena aamanoo wa 'amilus saalihaati falahum ajrun ghayru mamnoon

"Except for those who believe and do righteous deeds, for they will have a reward uninterrupted."

7

فَمَا يُكَذِّبُكَ بَعْدُ بِالدِّينِ ﴿٧﴾

Famaa yukazzibuka ba'du bid deen

"So what yet causes you to deny the Recompense [the Day of Judgement]?"

8

أَلَيْسَ اللَّهُ بِأَحْكَمِ الْحَاكِمِينَ ﴿٨﴾

Alaisal laahu bi-ahkamil haakimeen

"Is not Allah the most just of judges?"
